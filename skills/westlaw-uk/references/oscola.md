# OSCOLA output rules (4th edn)

Cite UK authorities OSCOLA-style. Westlaw's "Where Reported" list gives the
report series; pick in this order of preference: neutral citation, then Law
Reports (AC, QB/KB, Ch, Fam), then Session Cases (SC) for Scots cases, then WLR
/ All ER / SLT / SCLR, then specialist series.

## Cases

Pattern: *Party v Party* [year] Court Abbrev number, [year] Series page.

- *Donoghue v Stevenson* [1932] AC 562 (HL)
- *Cantiere San Rocco SA v Clyde Shipbuilding and Engineering Co Ltd* 1923 SC (HL) 105
- *Dow v Tayside University Hospitals NHS Trust* 2006 SLT (Sh Ct) 141
- *R (Miller) v Prime Minister* [2019] UKSC 41, [2020] AC 373

Scots cases reported in Session Cases use the year **without** square brackets
when the year is the volume. Neutral citations are always square-bracketed.
Pinpoint with the paragraph number in square brackets: `[2019] UKSC 41 [55]`,
or page: `1923 SC (HL) 105, 117`.

Add the status line after the citation when reporting from Westlaw:

> *Case* [cite]. Westlaw status: green tick (positive/neutral treatment), last cited 2024.

## Legislation

- Primary: *Prescription and Limitation (Scotland) Act 1973*, s 6(1).
- Secondary: *Civil Legal Aid (Scotland) Regulations 2002*, SSI 2002/494, reg 7.
- UK SIs: SI 2015/1392.
- Point in time: add "(as at 1 October 2026)" when the Westlaw version date matters.

## Journals

Author, 'Title' (year) volume Journal page.

- Hector MacQueen, 'Unjustified Enrichment in Scots Law' (2013) 17 Edin LR 33.

Westlaw's journal record gives the abbreviation; prefer the OSCOLA-standard
abbreviation if you know it, otherwise copy Westlaw's.

## General

- Italicise case names, not citations.
- No full stops in abbreviations (AC not A.C.).
- Pinpoints follow a comma for pages, no comma for paragraph brackets.
- If Westlaw shows only a Westlaw-specific citation (e.g. `[2024] 3 WLUK 123`), give it but flag it as a Westlaw UK index reference, not a report.
